## 1. Rules of the road — measurements (p.54–62)

These are the enumerable, testable numbers. Most are learner's-test staples.

### General (p.54)
- Drive on the **left** of a two-way road; the right of a one-way is permitted where safe.
- Safe following distance: **2 seconds** in a light motor vehicle, **3 seconds** in a heavy
  motor vehicle or truck. Increase in poor conditions.
- The vehicle **licence disc is valid for 12 months** and must always be displayed.
- A traffic officer's instructions **override** any road rule or traffic sign.

### Shoulder of the road (p.54)
May move onto the shoulder to let a vehicle overtake **only** when all three hold:
- no obstructing vehicle or pedestrian visible for at least **150 m** ahead;
- the road is **single-lane**;
- it is between **sunrise and sunset**.
Continuous driving on the shoulder is prohibited.

### Where you may NOT stop (p.60)
- Alongside an excavation, where it obstructs traffic flow.
- In a tunnel, subway or on a bridge — **or within 6 m** of one.
- On or **within 6 m** of a point where the roadway narrows.
- In contravention of any road sign or marking.
- On the **right-hand side** of the road facing oncoming traffic.
- Alongside or opposite another vehicle where the road is **less than 9 m** wide.
- Within the rail reserve at a level crossing.
- **Within 9 m of the approach side** of a pedestrian crossing.
- **Within 1,5 m of a fire hydrant.**
- Anywhere it causes an obstruction; on a freeway.

### Where you may NOT park (p.55) — in addition to all of the above
- Where you would obscure a road sign.
- On the sidewalk (unless a street vendor/hawker vehicle).
- At the entrance to any private or public road.
- **Within 1 m of the road edge outside an urban area**, unless in a marked bay.
- **Within 1,5 m of a fire hydrant.**
- **Within 9 m of a pedestrian crossing.**
- **Within 5 m of an intersection.**
- **Upon the actuating mechanism of a traffic signal.**
- With the **left wheels more than 450 mm** from the pavement edge, unless a sign permits.
- On a road **less than 5,5 m wide**, unless one-way.
- On a traffic island, in a pedestrian lane or pedestrian mall; in a loading zone.

Exempt: emergency vehicles, on-duty police and traffic officers, road construction and
maintenance vehicles. Penalty: fine and/or impoundment.

### Compulsory stops (p.60)
Always stop when: directed by a traffic officer or road sign; involved in an accident; a
pedestrian is about to enter a pedestrian crossing; requested by a person herding animals
across the road.

### Overtaking (p.56–57)
- Overtake **on the right** unless prohibited by sign or marking; return left only when
  safely past.
- Never where visibility is limited (blind rise, corner/curve), into oncoming traffic, on
  a solid or double white line, or at/approaching a pedestrian crossing.
- Never overtake a vehicle that has **stopped at a pedestrian crossing**.
- Passing a stationary bus: check for boarding/alighting passengers.
- Overtaking **on the left** is permitted only where the vehicle ahead is turning right, or
  where there are two or more lanes in the same direction — never crossing the yellow line.
- **Being overtaken**: move left if safe and hold your speed — do not accelerate.

### General duties of drivers (p.61) — prohibited
Reversing longer than necessary; following closer than is safe; letting a person, animal or
load hamper control (including observation, hearing, signalling); letting another person
steer; leaving the vehicle without the handbrake set; any part of the body protruding
(except for hand signals); a person or animal on the roof or running board while moving;
excessive smoke or fumes; leaving the engine running while the vehicle is unattended **or
while fuel is being pumped**; depositing fuel/oil/grease/ashes/refuse on the road; anyone
entering or leaving while moving; driving/pushing/pulling a vehicle on the sidewalk;
**operating a communication device without a hands-free kit**.
Always give way to a vehicle sounding a siren when safe. Loud music/headphones discouraged.

### Motorcycles, tricycles, quad-bikes (p.62)
- Sit astride the saddle, **both feet on the footrests**.
- **No passenger if engine capacity is 50 cc or less.**
- No more than one passenger behind you; two only with a suitable side-car.
- No passenger or bulky object in front of you.
- Ride **single file with headlights on**.
- Helmet for rider **and** passenger at all times.
- Two or more motorcycles may not overtake the same vehicle simultaneously.
- **At least one hand on the handlebars** at all times; all wheels in contact with the road.
- Load may not project more than **600 mm to the front, 900 mm to the rear, 450 mm to the
  side**.
- May not hold on to another vehicle, be towed, or tow.

### Noise, pedestrians, racing (p.62)
- The **hooter must be audible at 90 m**; may not be used unnecessarily.
- Pedestrian crossings: drivers yield to a pedestrian entering or already on the crossing;
  a pedestrian may not step out so as to make yielding difficult; where the crossing sits at
  a traffic light, the pedestrian obeys the light. **No overtaking a vehicle stopped at a
  crossing.**
- Any race or sporting event on a public road needs authority approval.

### Hindering traffic, abandoned vehicles, road damage (p.63)
- You may not hinder the free flow of traffic with your vehicle **or any other object**.
- A vehicle is treated as **abandoned** — and will be removed and impounded — when it is
  causing an obstruction; parked in a prohibited area; **left standing more than 24 hours on
  a public road outside an urban area**; **left standing more than 7 days on a public road
  within an urban area**; has no licence or registration number; or is parked in a
  no-stopping or no-parking area.
- Damage to public roads: no wheelspinning or dragging your wheels; no chocks or shoes
  between the wheels; no damage to the road surface.

### Freeways (p.64)
- **You may drive on a freeway on a learner's licence, provided you are accompanied by
  someone holding a valid driving licence of the same category.**
- If a vehicle behind wishes to overtake, indicate and move to the left lane when safe.
- **Not permitted on a freeway**: animal-drawn vehicles · bicycles · **motorcycles of 50 cc
  or less** · motor tricycles and quadrucycles · **vehicles of 230 kg or less specifically
  constructed for disabled people** · tractors · pedestrians (unless in an area reserved for
  stopping, or in a situation beyond their control) · animals (unless in a vehicle or in a
  reserved area).
- **You may not stop on a freeway unless** instructed by a traffic officer, in an area
  reserved for stopping or parking, or in a situation beyond your control.
- **Hand signals may not be used on a freeway** unless in a situation beyond your control.

### Towing (p.65)
- Tow-rope, tow-bar or chain between the vehicles may not exceed **3,5 m**.
- A licensed driver must control the towed vehicle unless its front wheels are clear of the
  ground or a device controls the steering.
- Defective brakes on the towed vehicle ⇒ it must be coupled with an appropriate **tow-bar**.
- A draw-bar or tow-bar is required to tow **in excess of 30 km/h**.
- **No passengers in a towed vehicle**, unless that vehicle is a semi-trailer.
- Motorcycles, tricycles and quadrucycles may not be used for towing.

### Speed limits (p.65)
- **60 km/h** urban · **100 km/h** outside urban · **120 km/h** freeway.
- **80 km/h** for a goods or combination vehicle exceeding **9 000 kg**.
- **100 km/h** for a bus or mini-bus.

### Seat belts (p.65)
- Worn at all times by driver and passengers, **except while reversing or parking**.
- A child under **14** must sit in the back **unless taller than 1,5 m**.
- An adult may not sit in a beltless seat if a belted seat is available.
- Belts fitted to the vehicle must be in good working order.
- **Infants under 3** must be in an appropriate child restraint — not applicable to public
  transport vehicles.

### Tyres (p.65)
- Tread must be visible and at least **1 mm** deep over the entire surface.
- Motorcycles of **50 cc or less**: visible tread over no less than **80%** of the surface.
- Motorcycles over 50 cc: at least 1 mm over the entire surface.
- Good condition, no bulges or visible canvas. **Retread tyres may not be used on
  motorcycles.**

### Lights (p.66)
- Hazard lights only in an emergency, or when stopped in a hazardous position.
- **Motorcyclists: headlights on at all times.** Other vehicles: between sunset and sunrise,
  and whenever visibility is less than **150 m**.
- Main beams should illuminate objects up to **100 m** ahead; **dipped beam should not
  illuminate the road more than 45 m ahead**.
- Main beams only where there is no oncoming traffic.
- **Parking lights** only when parked outside a parking bay, or in a dark area **more than
  12 m from a streetlight**.
- **Adjustable spotlights** may be used only by doctors, vets, at breakdowns, and by official
  vehicles.

### Accidents (p.66)
In any accident you must: stop immediately · establish whether anyone is injured and call the
police if so · give what assistance you can · provide your name and address, the vehicle's
registration number and the owner's name and address · **report to the nearest police station
within 24 hours, with your driving licence on you**.
- **You may not take alcohol or drugs before reporting the accident.**
- If there are injuries the vehicle may be moved only with an official's authorisation and
  only after its position has been marked.
- If nobody is injured you may move the vehicle if it is obstructing traffic.

### Alcohol (p.66)
- Ordinary drivers: below **0,05 g per 100 ml** of blood, and **0,24 mg per 1 000 ml** of
  breath.
- Professional drivers: **0,02 g per 100 ml** and **0,10 mg per 1 000 ml**.

### Intersections, circles, turning (p.58–59)
- Do not enter an intersection if you will obstruct flow when the signal changes.
- **Turning left**: keep as far left as possible; correct lane per direction arrows.
- **Turning right**: keep as far right as possible; yield to oncoming traffic; allow vehicles
  behind to pass on your left; pass the crossroad's centre line before turning; do not cut
  the corner.
- **Mini-circles and roundabouts**: yield to the right; vehicles **already in** the circle have
  right of way; **at a mini-circle, whoever crosses the yield line first has right of way**;
  always indicate the exit you intend to take.
- Hand signals **may not be used on a freeway**.

## 2. Vehicle controls (p.101–113)

### Foot controls (p.103)
- **Accelerator**: right foot only; press lightly while engaging the clutch when moving off;
  no up-and-down foot movement; smooth and gradual.
- **Foot brake**: right foot alternates between brake and accelerator; never slam the pedal
  (locks the brakes, loses control); if the brakes lock, **release and re-apply gently**;
  complete braking **before** turning where possible; both hands stay on the wheel while
  braking; **do not change to a lower gear to assist braking**.
- **Clutch**: left foot exclusively, and nowhere else; push in fast, release slowly; never
  rest the foot on the pedal; anchoring the left heel to the floor aids control; when slowing
  for a corner the order is **brake first, then clutch**.

### Hand controls (p.104)
- Testing in an **automatic restricts you to automatics**; testing in a manual licenses both.
- Neutral sits between the **3rd and 4th** gear positions.
- Depress the clutch before every gear change; move through the neutral passage into 1st or
  reverse; practise changing without looking; don't rest a hand on the lever; don't change
  gear through a sharp corner.
- Unattended automatic: lever in **PARK**. Manufacturers often advise leaving a manual in
  **first or reverse** when parked on an incline.
- **Parking brake**: used when the vehicle is left unattended, on inclines and at complete
  stops. During the test it must stop the vehicle rolling — rolling is instant failure. Pull
  it up with **the thumb button pressed in so the ratchet does not click**. Never apply it at
  speed (skid risk). Hands stay on the wheel until fully stopped, then apply. It goes up
  **before** the lever goes to neutral. To release: lift slightly, press the button fully,
  lower to the floor, release the button last.

### Steering (p.105, 110, 113)
- **Push-pull ("shuffle") method** only, so both hands stay on the wheel.
- Hands at **"ten to two" or "quarter to three"** — never the lower half of the wheel.
- **Both thumbs on the inside rim.**
- Never let the wheel spin back to centre itself; never release both hands at once.
- Don't turn the wheel while stationary (tyre and mechanism wear); don't grip too tightly.
- Sequence: pull down the right side while the left hand slides to the **"six thirty"**
  position, then the left hand pushes up while the right returns to **twelve o'clock**.

### Indicators (p.105)
- Signal **roughly 100 m, or 5 seconds**, before the turn — unless a vehicle is emerging from
  a side road and it would be unsafe.
- Signal only in the direction you intend to go; cancel immediately after a slight turn that
  does not self-cancel.
- Operate with the fingers; keep the hand on the wheel.
- Indicate before moving off forwards or backwards from stationary.

### Lights, hooter, mirrors (p.106)
- Check front and rear lights **at least daily**; replace failed bulbs/broken lenses promptly.
- Lights on **between sunset and sunrise**, and in poor weather when road users are not
  clearly visible **within 150 m**.
- Brights only when there is no oncoming traffic.
- Hooter: audible at **90 m**; gives no right of way; a warning device only; not outside
  hospitals or churches unless unavoidable; short burst only when overtaking pedestrians or
  cyclists; check the mirror before hooting.
- **Centre (rear-view) mirror** is the main mirror scored in the K53 parking and driving test
   — keep its field of view clear. Side mirrors are optional on a light motor vehicle but
  cover blind spots; set them, then have someone walk around the vehicle to learn where the
  blind spots begin (p.121 diagram: blind spots sit left and right, behind the door pillars).
- Wipers: replace roughly every **six months**; wet the glass before wiping off dirt/sand/salt.

### Starting procedure, 13 steps (p.108)
1 Parking brake engaged · 2 Gear in neutral · 3 Final seat and mirror adjustment · 4 All
warning/ignition lights off · 5 Key to **ON** without starting · 6 Confirm warning lights and
gauges work · 7 Choke if fitted and the engine is cold · 8 Turn further until the starter
engages, then a gentle push on the accelerator · 9 **Release the key the instant it starts**
(or you ruin the starter motor) · 10 Watch the lights and gauges; check fuel · 11 Seat belt on
— **you are not compelled to wear a belt while reversing** · 12 Ensure passengers can use and
release their belts · 13 Cancel a manual choke if it over-revs.

### Moving off, stopping, reversing (p.107–109)
- Clutch fully in, first gear; accelerator to about **1 000 r/min**; ease the clutch out until
  the revs drop to about **750 r/min** (the biting point — the rear drags or the bonnet lifts);
  then release the handbrake and move off. Keep the heel on the floor for clutch control.
- **Stopping**: mirror first → release the accelerator smoothly → gentle brake, moving in a
  straight line → clutch in near the stop → ease brake pressure off as it halts → re-apply the
  brake immediately to prevent movement. Both hands on the wheel until stationary.
- **Reversing**: neutral, handbrake on, engine running → clutch to the floor → select reverse
  → look behind **by turning to your left and looking out of the rear window** (wider field
  than mirrors) → move slowly. Guide with the **right hand on top of the wheel**.

## 3. Pre-trip inspection (p.114–116)

The examiner **starts the clock** at the exterior check. Inspect **anti-clockwise, top to
bottom**. If the vehicle is judged unroadworthy at any point you **fail immediately and never
reach the interior check**. You may point at an item and say "I must check this" rather than
touching it.

**Exterior — the book's 30 numbered points, in order:**
1 Look **underneath** for leaks or obstructions (also the only way to see a child or animal
under the car) · 2 Unlock the driver's door, then the rest from inside · 3 Begin the
walk-around anti-clockwise · 4 Driver's side mirror unbroken, stable, clean · 5 Driver's side
of the windscreen · 6 **Lift the driver's wiper, feel the blade, say "not torn or perished",
leave it up** · 7 Front right tyre — pressure, sidewall damage, tread depth, nuts tight ·
8 Front right light — lens and reflector · 9 Bonnet and bumper secure · 10 State you have
checked **oil, water, brake fluid and engine belts** · 11 Front registration plate ·
12 Front left light · 13 Front left tyre (as the first) · 14 Passenger side of the windscreen ·
15 Lift the passenger wiper, same statement, leave it up · 16 Licence disc validity ·
17 Left exterior mirror · 18 Left doors and window winders · 19 Seat belts for stability and
damage · 20 Information plate (bus/goods vehicle) · 21 Fuel cap secure and not leaking (do
this after step 29 if the cap is on the right) · 22 Left rear tyre · 23 Left rear light lens ·
24 Boot secure; mention spare wheel, jack and spanner · 25 Rear bumper and exhaust secure ·
26 Chevron where applicable · 27 Rear registration lamp then plate · 28 Right rear light lens ·
29 Right rear tyre · 30 Right doors and window winders.

**Interior** — the examiner asks you to operate: (1) lights, bright and dim, checking front,
rear and stop lights; (2) indicators both sides, front and rear; (3) wipers in the extended
position; (4) the hooter. Any failure here is likely an unroadworthy fail.

Then, with the examiner seated, **you say aloud**: "All the doors are closed" · "The parking
brake is in the engaged position" · "The gear is in neutral" (or "park") · "There are no
obstructions on the floor or anything blocking my view through the front or back window."

## 4. The yard test (p.113, 117–125)

- The yard test is the **first phase** of the driver's test: pre-trip inspection (exterior and
  interior), alley docking, parallel parking, turn in the road, incline start.
- **The whole yard test must be completed within 20 minutes.**
- **Yard test total: 50 points.**
- **There is no fixed sequence** — the examiner may start with any manoeuvre.
- Anything marked by a **black box** on the test sheet stops the test immediately.

### Sequence checks — "SIM" (p.119–120)
Performed in the painted box before every movement:
1. **Stop** — parking brake immediately after stopping completely; gear to neutral; both feet
   on the floor and relax.
2. **Indicate** — **360° observation**, done slowly enough for the examiner to see it; then
   indicate the direction you intend to move.
3. **Move** — select gear; get clutch control (stops the vehicle rolling the wrong way); a
   second **360° observation**; keep a hand on the parking brake through the clutch control
   and release it immediately after the observation; move off slowly.

### Scored items and their point values

Common to the manoeuvres:

| Code | Meaning | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Roll | Vehicle rolls | **Immediate termination** |
| Obstacles | Any part of the vehicle touches a pole | **Immediate disqualification** |
| M. kerb | **Mounts** the kerb (wheel lifts onto the pavement) | **Immediate disqualification** |
| No. mve | More than 3 movements in the turn in the road | **Immediate disqualification** |
| P. br | Parking brake not used, or out of sequence | 2 |
| P. br. rat | Ratchet heard (thumb button not pressed) | 1 |
| Obs | 360° observation missed | 5 |
| Sig | Signal missed when turning during any parking stage | 5 |
| Sig. can | Signal left on after the manoeuvre | 4 |
| Gear | Wrong gear | 1 |
| Move | Moves too quickly or jerkily | 1 |
| Stall | Engine cuts out (restart with the full starting procedure) | 1 |
| Count. st | Counter-steer (straightening the front wheels) not done | 1 |
| B. kerb | **Bumps** the kerb (wheel or tyre contacts it) | 4 |
| N/D | Neutral (manual) / Drive (automatic) on the incline | 1 |

- **No. att** — a **second attempt** is allowed at a parking manoeuvre provided you did not
  roll and did not touch an obstacle.

### Alley docking (p.123)
- Begin from painted box **X**; both hands on the wheel; parking brake; sequence checks;
  neutral; relax with feet off the pedals. The examiner then directs you to reverse between
  the poles.
- **Two attempts allowed.** You may stop as often as needed — **if stopped for more than three
  seconds, do a 360° check** before moving again.
- Reverse until the vehicle is close to pole **A**, sweep-check back to area **Z**, then steer
  around the poles keeping them roughly **half a metre** from the side of the vehicle.
- **Stop with pole B in line with your head.** Repeat from the opposite direction.
- Touching any pole is instant disqualification.

### Parallel parking (p.124)
- Get in within **three movements**: (i) reverse in; (ii) move forward; (iii) reverse straight
  back until **the white dot on the ground is in line with your shoulder**.
- **Completing it in a single movement is equally acceptable.**
- Start in box **X** with the side of the vehicle about **half a metre from pole A** and the
  rear well past the poles.
- Reverse looking at the poles; when pole **A** appears in the side window nearest the back,
  sweep-check to area **Z** and lock the wheel towards pole A; watch the right mirror and when
  the third pole **F** appears, turn the wheel the opposite way to full lock; stop only when
  both right-hand corners are well past poles **A** and **D**.
- Parallel is the goal, but anywhere inside the designated area between the poles is a pass.
- May not touch a pole; may not mount the kerb; may not roll.

### Turn in the road / three-point turn (p.122, 125)
- **Three movements maximum — a fourth is immediate disqualification.**
- Stop as close to the left kerb as possible in the demarcated box; sequence checks; move
  forward with full right lock; when about **one metre** from the kerb or roughly at **90°**
  to it, **counter-steer** and stop.
- Sequence checks again; reverse with quick full left lock; once at about **45°**, or the
  steering wheel position is past the road's centre line, counter-steer and stop **before
  touching the kerb**.
- Sequence checks again; move forward, parallel to the opposite kerb, stop with the centre
  line in full view from the driver's window; engage the parking brake.

### Incline start (p.119, 125)
- Stop behind the line, both hands on the wheel, engage the parking brake, gear to **neutral**
  (manual) or **Drive** after neutral (automatic) — this proves the parking brake works.
- 360° check → first gear → clutch control → **second 360° check** → release the parking brake
  → as it moves, ease the accelerator down while releasing the clutch.
- **Any rearward roll is immediate failure.**

## 5. The road test (p.126–140)

Penalties may be **allocated more than once** — each occurrence adds to the total.

### Starting
P. br 2 · P. br. rat 1 · N/P 1 · Choke 1 · **Str. eng 1 — one point every time the engine
fails to start first time** · Warn. lts/Gauges 1 (key to ON, watch the lights until the engine
starts).

### Moving off
Obs 5 (compulsory 360° **before** indicating) · Sig 5 · Gear 1 · **W.T.L. 1 — waits too long,
failing to use a safe gap** · Move 1 · Stall 1 · **Roll = automatic failure; the test is
terminated** · Sig. can 4.

### Steering
Method 1 (push-pull) · **Obs 5 — you must check your mirror every 5–8 seconds while driving,
and lose five points each time you don't** · **Wide/Cut 4** — turning left over the middle of
the road, turning right so wide you almost bump the far kerb, cutting corners, taking a left
too soon, or cutting over the centre line on a right turn · Wand 2 (wanders) · Pos 2
(positioning) · Strad 2 (straddling markings).

### Clutch and gears
Clutch: Sm/co 1 · Slips 1 · **Rides 1** (foot resting on the pedal, or held down at a stop) ·
**Coasts 3** (clutch held in too long, or coasting in before braking).
Gear changing: Gear 1 (wrong gear, labouring, **or failing to use a lower gear downhill**) ·
Sm/co 1 · **Eyes 5 — looking down at the lever** · **Whilst cnr 4 — changing gear while
cornering** · **Coasts 3 — selecting neutral before stopping ("gear coasting")**.

### Signalling and hand signals
Mir 3 · **Bl. sp 5 — the right blind spot must be checked before the arm goes out** ·
Hand R / Hand L / Hand S 3 each · Horn 1.
- Hand signals must be complete **before** braking, from at least **100 m or roughly 12
  seconds** out.
- **Two attempts** are allowed; a mistake on the second attempt fails the test.
- Right turn: arm fully out and horizontal, palm forward, fingers together.
- Left turn: right arm out horizontally, forearm pointing at the ground, rotated
  **anti-clockwise**.
- Stop: arm out horizontally, forearm straight up, palm open and facing forward.
- Slowing down: arm out horizontally, forearm down, moved up and down.
- Mirror/blind-spot check before **any** hand signal; check mirrors again before slowing;
  another blind-spot check immediately before turning.

### Lane changing / overtaking / intersections
Lane changing: Mir 3 · Bl. sp 5 · Sig 5 · Sig. can 4.
Overtaking: Mir 3 · Bl. sp 5 · Sig 5 · Sig. can 4 · **Cl. sp 5** (clear space). After
overtaking, **repeat the full sequence** to return to your original side.
Intersections / vehicle entrances: Mir 3 · Bl. sp 5 · Sig 5 · Ln. chng 3 · **Ch. R/L 5**
(look right and left) · **Pos. turn 4** (close to the centre line, vehicle straight) ·
**Wheels 3 — front wheels must point straight ahead while waiting to turn**, so a rear-end
shunt does not push you into oncoming traffic · Sig. can 4.

### Speed control
Mir 3 · **Fast 5** (not reducing speed for hazards even within the limit) · Slow 1 · Acc 1 ·
Dec 1 · Br 2 (**gears may not be used to assist braking**) · **Fol. dist 5 — the two-second
rule, counted as "1001, 1002".**

### Stopping
Mir 3 · Bl. sp 5 · Sig 5 · Br 2 (straight line; hands stay on the wheel until stationary) ·
Dis. cl 1 · Cl. sp 2 · P. br 2 · P. br. rat 1 · N/D/P 1 · Sig. can 4 · **Needless 1** (stopping
without reason).

### Freeways
Mir 3 · Bl. sp 5 · Sig 5 · Sig. can 4 · Cl. sp 5.
- Start the checks at least **100 m or 12 seconds** before entering or exiting.
- **50 m before the actual entrance or exit, check both blind spots again.**
- Passing an **on-ramp**: check the **left** blind spot. Passing an **off-ramp**: check
  **both**.
- Examiners generally prefer you blend using the side verge rather than stopping.
- Do not travel in the right-hand lane except to overtake.

### Emergency stop
**Stop 5** · **Con. stop 5** (must stay in a straight line; if the brakes lock, release and
re-apply) · **Two attempts only** — a second failure fails the test.

### Instant failures on the road test
- Breaking **any** rule of the road — the test is terminated on the spot and you are not
  necessarily told until you are back at the test centre.
- **Uncontrolled/dangerous actions**: manoeuvring dangerously around pedestrians, taking
  unsafe gaps, or turning corners uncontrollably fast.
- **Causing an accident.** If someone else causes it you may continue at the examiner's
  discretion, provided the vehicle is still roadworthy. A **mechanical breakdown is not a
  failure** — the result is recorded as **Deferred**.
- Rolling even slightly at a stop line.
- Failing to obey a road sign.

### Signals, signs and intersections on the road test (p.138–140)
- Stop **behind** the white line; where there is no marking, stop with the front of the
  vehicle behind the sign.
- **Steady amber: slow down and stop.** Never drive through it unless stopping safely behind
  the line is impossible.
- **Flashing red = treat as a stop sign. Flashing amber = treat as a yield sign.**
- A steady or flashing **green arrow gives right of way even when the signal is red**,
  provided the intersection is clear.
- **Uncontrolled intersection** (no signal, stop, yield or circle): check mirrors, look right
  and left, slow if necessary, proceed when safe.
- Four-way stop: give way to whoever stopped first; **if uncertain, yield to the right**.
- Turning right: yield to oncoming traffic whether it is turning or going straight. Turning
  left: you have right of way.
- Traffic circles: yield to vehicles already in the circle **and** to those approaching from
  the right. **Leaving by the first exit — enter with the left indicator already on. Leaving
  by a later exit — signal only once you are inside the circle.**
- **Level crossing: stop at least 5 m from the nearest rail.**
- Emergency vehicles always have right of way. Pedestrians always have right of way at
  crossings and at scholar patrols.
- Lane changing is not permitted unless the examiner asks, or a sign/marking requires it
  (merge, road ends). Do not exceed the speed limit to take a gap.

## 6. The eye test (p.96)

The manual prints a Snellen chart, to be read **standing 3 m from the page**, with both eyes
open, then each eye in turn. Rows, with the manual's own "visual acuity loss" column:

| Acuity | Loss |
|---|---|
| 6/60 | 80% |
| 6/36 | 55% |
| 6/24 | 40% |
| 6/18 | 30% |
| 6/12 | 15% |
| 6/9 | 10% |
| 6/6 | 0% |
| 6/5 | — |

Rows from 6/18 down mix in **reversed E glyphs (Ǝ)** — the tumbling-E optotype the DLTC
screening machine uses. The manual's advice: if you cannot read the bottom line clearly,
with either eye, see an optometrist.

Used as the ladder and scoring basis for `src/lib/vision/acuity.ts`.

## 7. Conflicts to resolve

1. **Yard test time limit.** This manual says **20 minutes** (p.113).
   `docs/content/facts/controls-yard.md` says **20 min 59 s**, sourced from the DoT K53
   document and k53sim. Both are in circulation; 20:59 appears to be the official allowance
   including its grace second. **Do not author a question that turns on the exact figure**
   until one is confirmed against the current DoT K53 manual.

2. **Steering method naming.** This manual calls it **"push-pull"** / "shuffle";
   `controls-yard.md` records **"pull–push"**. Same technique, and the K53 sequence in this
   manual is pull-then-push. Prefer "push-pull (shuffle)" and avoid making the word order
   itself the answer.

4. **Broken-line road markings — `marking-086-02/03/04`.** The catalogue captions these
   continuity line / lane line / dividing line, in that order. Rendered at 2×, the images run
   sparsest → densest in the same order: 086-02 has short marks with long gaps, 086-04 has
   marks and gaps roughly equal. That is the **reverse** of the SARTSM convention as
   understood — a lane line (same-direction separation) is the sparse one and a dividing
   line (opposing traffic) is denser, with the continuity line denser still. Either the
   captions are shuffled across those three images or the convention is misremembered.
   **Left unnamed until checked against SARTSM Volume 2 (`V2C1.pdf`).** Naming them wrong
   would teach three confusable markings under three wrong names, which is worse than
   teaching none.

5. **`marking-082-02` — diamond marking.** Captioned "Lane reserved for hazardous goods
   vehicles only", but a diamond is the generic exclusive-use lane symbol in South Africa
   (and `084-05` / `085-01..04` are all captioned "Exclusive use lane symbol"). Possible
   caption misattribution; unnamed pending the same source check.

6. **Gears and braking.** p.103 says flatly "do not change to a lower gear to assist
   braking", and the road-test sheet penalises using gears to assist braking (Br, 2 points).
   But the Gear Changing sheet penalises **not** using a lower gear **when driving downhill**
   (Gear, 1 point). These are consistent — engine braking is for sustained descents, not for
   ordinary stops — but a question must not conflate them. Existing items in
   `controls-extra-pack.ts` handle the descent case correctly.
