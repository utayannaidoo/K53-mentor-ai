processed into `public/audio/px-*.wav` by `scripts/build-sfx.mjs`.

| Cue file | Source | Author | Pixabay ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| `px-click.wav` | Soft Interface Click | Universfield | 126517 |
| `px-chime.wav` | Clean Ding Beep | Virtual_Vibes | 383726 |
| `px-glass.wav` | UI Success Chime | SoundShelfStudio | 513565 |
| `px-type.wav` | Keyboard Typing SFX | SoundReality | 525007 |

`px-tick.wav` is built by the same script but **not wired in**. The Pixabay tick
centres at 792 Hz against the synthesised tick's 3400 Hz, and swapping it cost
10–12 dB of brightness on every card in the fifteen-card deal. Fifteen dull
thuds in three seconds reads as mud. Realism is not the goal for a sound that
marks an abstraction rather than a physical object.

## Still synthesised

`impact`, `impact-soft`, `impact-huge`, `shatter`, `rise-tone` are still
generated by `scripts/synth-audio.mjs`. Pixabay's impact category is dominated
by cinematic-trailer material — braams, boulder thuds, long reverb tails —
which is the opposite of what this film wants. The synthesised hits are dry and
tonal and currently measure 15–34 dB over the score, so they are not the
problem. Replace them only with something specifically clean and dry.

## Why the processing step exists

`scripts/build-sfx.mjs` does three things, each for a measured reason:

1. **Trims leading silence.** The raw downloads start with 24–325 ms of padding
   before the sound begins. At 60 fps that is up to 20 frames. This cue sheet
   places sounds to the frame, so untrimmed files land visibly late. After
   processing every file starts within 0.3 of a frame.
2. **Normalises peaks.** Raw peaks ranged −0.0 to −14.8 dBFS. Every `volume`
   in `cues.ts` is tuned against a known level, so normalising means those
   numbers keep meaning the same thing. `px-type` is held 6 dB lower than the
   one-shots because it runs under a whole scene rather than marking a frame.
3. **Fades the tail 8 ms.** Several cues are `frames`-bounded and would
   otherwise be cut mid-waveform, which clicks.

## Score

Still `bed-seamless.wav`, cut from Suno generations — see `MUSIC.md`. If you
replace it, `MUSIC.volume` in `cues.ts` is the one knob to retune first, and
the track needs a quiet passage around 0:16–0:19 for the silence before the
drop.
